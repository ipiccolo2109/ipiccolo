# Graduation Countdown

A lightweight single-page countdown timer for May 9, 2026.

## Open it in your browser

- Open `index.html` in your browser, or
- run `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder and then open <http://localhost:8000> in your browser.

The timer updates automatically and shows the remaining days, hours, and minutes until graduation day in your local time.
